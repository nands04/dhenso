Frequently asked questions

Can I manage my schedule, estimates, and invoices in one place?

Yes, the platform operates as a complete all-in-one CRM specifically designed for field service management. You can seamlessly convert an approved customer estimate into a scheduled job, assign it to an available crew member, and instantly generate a final invoice. This centralizes your entire workflow, ensuring no job details or payments fall through the cracks.

What kind of automations and customer reminders can I send?

The system minimizes no-shows and administrative work by automating your customer communication. You can trigger automatic SMS and email appointment confirmations, 24-hour arrival reminders, and follow-up templates. Additionally, you can set up automated review requests that deploy immediately after a technician marks a job as complete.

How do my field technicians access their daily job routes?

Your field workers receive access to a dedicated mobile app designed specifically to track their daily tasks and progress. Technicians can view their assigned schedule, pull up customer notes, get optimized routing to the next site, and upload before-and-after photos. This keeps the back office updated with live progress statuses and syncs perfectly with your dispatcher's dashboard.
